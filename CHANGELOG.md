# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-30

First packaged preview of Beam for macOS 26 and later.

- First-launch introduction and TypeSafe account connection, with a plain-reader option.
- Explicit Connect and Disconnect controls, secure key entry and actionable connection errors.
- Key validation and reliable Keychain storage before switching accounts.
- Pinned-search catch-up after reconnecting or interrupted refreshes.
- A bounded judging queue that starts with the strongest search candidates.
- Adaptive article-body skeleton while a page loads, with accessible loading status.
- Persisted Yellow, Green, Blue, Purple and Pink highlight palettes in Settings, with adaptive reader and list marks.
- RSS/Atom and built-in sources, search by meaning, article reading and matching passages.
- Versioned universal app packaging, release checksum and automated offline checks.

The download is ad-hoc signed and is not Apple notarized.
